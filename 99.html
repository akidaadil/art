99%

Emotional Thresholds in Female Self-Representation

2023 – 2026
Photography & Sound Practice

This project explores the emotional dissonance between external perception and internal self-awareness among young women, translating subjective emotional states into two parallel systems: photographic portraiture and musical notation.

Through pre-shoot interviews, emotional keyword mapping, graphic waveform sketches, solo violin transcription, and controlled portrait framing, subjective experiences are translated into visual and sonic forms. Sound emerges as a performed interpretation of these emotional states.

Positioned between documentation and abstraction, the project constructs a space in which image and sound operate as parallel yet interdependent narrative structures rather than illustrative elements. Sound functions as an autonomous emotional register, existing alongside the photographic image.
