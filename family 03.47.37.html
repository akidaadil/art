Female Agency and Cultural Continuity
within Uyghur Society

Uyghur society has historically been shaped by Islamic traditions that influence family structure, gender expectations, and social roles. Across different historical periods — from the 1960s to the present — education, urban development, and global exposure have gradually reshaped women’s access to mobility and self-determination. Through three generations of women in my family, this project examines how female identity evolves within a culturally and religiously structured environment. The focus is not on opposition to tradition, but on how women reinterpret cultural frameworks from within.

A right-to-left bound book integrates Uyghur ornamental structures, embedding cultural logic into material form.
