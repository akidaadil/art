From Cinematic Gaze to Algorithmic Surveillance

2022–2025
Self-portraiture

This project examines the transformation of the gaze—from cinematic authorship to algorithmic and networked visibility in the age of AI.

Context

While Cindy Sherman addressed the male gaze embedded in cinema and photography, contemporary visual culture operates under a decentralized but intensified system of observation shaped by social media, AI generation, and image circulation.
