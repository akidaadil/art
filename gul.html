Gül

Belonging as Reconstruction

2025

This work approaches belonging not as origin, but as process. Drawing from Uyghur Muqam as both structural rhythm and emotional architecture, the project translates cycles of tension and resolution into a responsive digital field. Fragmented particles drift without orientation until sound and gesture intervene. Identity here is not given—it is continuously negotiated. Belonging does not precede fragmentation. It emerges through reconstruction.

Muqam is a classical Uyghur musical suite recognized by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage. Its structure unfolds through cycles of rise and fall, fragmentation and return. In this work, Muqam functions not as accompaniment but as generative architecture. Sound frequency directly shapes particle behavior, density, and spatial organization. Music becomes structure.

Using hand-tracking technology, viewers influence dispersion and aggregation in real time. The hand operates both as interface and metaphor. As gestures guide particles toward cohesion, the system visualizes a shift from fragmentation toward formation. Belonging requires embodied agency.

In Uyghur naming traditions, “Gül” (flower) frequently appears in female names. The flower functions as both linguistic marker and cultural symbol. Within the system, scattered particles reorganize into floral geometries. The flower does not pre-exist. It emerges. Belonging is visualized as becoming.
