7-11 Diary

Micro Public Space in Rapidly Developing Seoul

2021
Photography

During one year of working in a convenience store in Seoul, I conducted an embedded observation within a 24-hour commercial environment. The store functions as a transitional public space—neither home nor workplace—where presence is temporary and interaction is transactional. As the cashier, I remained spatially fixed while customers continuously moved through the space. This asymmetry created a structural condition in which short exchanges and fragmentary narratives emerged. Rather than documenting emotional loneliness, the project examines how rapid urban development reshapes everyday encounters and produces proximity without sustained social bonds.
